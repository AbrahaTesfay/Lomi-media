# Brand and content guide (editable draft)

## Proposed brand

**Name:** Lomi Media & Communication

**Positioning:** An Africa-rooted, globally minded creative media and communications studio.

**Core message:** Ideas that move people.

**Supporting message:** Creative stories. Confident brands. Connections beyond borders.

**Voice:** clear, confident, warm, contemporary and culturally aware.

## Palette

- Deep Ink `#102525` — identity, footer and premium contrast
- Warm Ivory `#F6F4EE` — page backgrounds
- Apricot `#F2A572` — expressive highlights and calls to action
- Soft Mint `#C8F0D8` — freshness and visual balance
- Forest `#193D3A` — supporting emphasis

**Fonts:** Space Grotesk for headings; DM Sans for body. The website gracefully falls back to system fonts if Google Fonts is unavailable. Review web-font privacy implications before launch.

**Logo:** Original, editable SVG wordmark built for the starter. Have a designer review uniqueness and trademark viability before adopting as the final corporate mark.

## Pages and what to verify

1. Home — value proposition, expertise, work examples, approach, CTA.
2. Services — detailed capabilities; edit to match what your company truly offers.
3. Work — only concept examples now; do not present as client achievements.
4. About — mission, values and process; add real founders, team bios and photos when verified.
5. Contact — project inquiries; add actual company contact channels before launch.
6. Privacy — template only, requiring appropriate legal review and operational details.

## Suggested future assets

- High-quality company/team photography with permission and licensing
- Real project cover images and approved client quotes, credits and outcomes
- Approved brand logo files and social media banner templates
- Press kit, company profile PDF, relevant credentials and legal documents
- Actual international language translations (e.g. English, Amharic, Tigrinya) where the business needs them

**Avoid claiming:** certifications, awards, client results, geographic offices, company age, or partnerships that you have not verified.
